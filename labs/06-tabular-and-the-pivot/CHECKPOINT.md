# Checkpoint

Hint on the pivot: read your own `PROBLEM.md` and ask who actually runs this system. Is
there one system for everyone, or one per team?

1. What did calibration do to ECE, and what did it do to AUC? Why would a post-hoc
   probability map cost you ranking?
2. A model with excellent ECE and chance AUC — what is it doing?
3. List the four honest responses to a weak result. Which one did you take and what did it
   cost you?
4. Your per-project result is much better than your cross-project result. Have you improved
   the model, or changed the question? What can you now no longer claim?
5. What would a less honest engineer have done with the weak cross-project number, and what
   would have happened three months later?
