# Checkpoint

Hint: for step 7, count the distinct test names that failed at least once, twice — once over
every run, once over only the runs your gate trusts. Compare the two counts.

1. Name three ways a run can report failures that never happened.
2. Two of your verdicts match the same run. Which one gets reported, and why does the order
   matter to somebody reading the audit trail six months from now?
3. What fraction of a suite failing did you call implausible, and what is that number based on?
4. How many distinct failing tests does your project have before the gate, and after?
5. You are discarding a large share of runs. What real signal might you be discarding with
   them, and how would you find out?
