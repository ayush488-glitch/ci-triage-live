# Checkpoint

Hint: write out the two rerun outcomes and ask, for each, "what set of worlds is now
excluded?"

1. A rerun flips the result. What does that prove?
2. A rerun does not flip. What does that prove?
3. Which of your two classes contains cases that are mislabelled, and in which direction?
4. What would it cost to buy enough certainty to trust the negative class?
5. If the negative class is contaminated, is your measured performance an over-estimate or
   an under-estimate of the truth? Defend your answer.
