# Checkpoint

Hint: ask what the system should output when the evidence genuinely does not distinguish
between two causes, and guessing would be worse than saying so.

If you can answer these, you are done:

1. What case do three outputs fail to cover, and what does the fourth output do about it?
2. Why is the fourth output cheap rather than free?
3. Which of your two main mistakes is worse in your organisation, and by roughly what factor?
4. What is the quantity the system minimises, written as a sentence?
5. What does accuracy assume about your cost table that you have just written down is false?
