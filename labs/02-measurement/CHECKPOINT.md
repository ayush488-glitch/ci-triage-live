# Checkpoint

Hint: for the binning question, work out how many of your samples land in the lowest bin
when only 3% are positive and most predicted probabilities are near zero.

If you can answer these, you are done:

1. How can 94% accuracy coexist with zero recall on the same predictions?
2. What question does ROC AUC answer that precision and recall do not?
3. What does ECE tell you, and what does it refuse to tell you?
4. Two models: one has near-zero ECE and chance-level AUC; the other has good AUC and poor
   ECE. Which would you rather ship, and what would you do to it first?
5. Why does allowing the system to abstain require a second axis on the plot?
