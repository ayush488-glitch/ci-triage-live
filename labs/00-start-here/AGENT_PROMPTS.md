# Prompts to adapt

These are starting points. Change them. An unedited prompt gets a generic answer.

```text
I am a developer on call. A build just went red on the release branch at 2:47am
with a single failing test and an AssertionError. Do not propose a solution.
List every distinct thing that could have caused this, and for each one, what
the right response would be.
```

```text
Here is my draft PROBLEM.md. Do not improve the writing. Tell me which of my four
answers describes a prediction and which describes a decision, and where I have
mixed them up.
```

```text
I wrote that a wrong answer costs "engineering time". Push back on that. What
would I need to know to put a number on it, and who in a real company would
know it?
```
