# Support depth

Use the phase's `level` as a hint for how much structure to offer. It describes the work's
depth, not a score.

## Level 1 — orient

Help the builder name the decision, actor, cost, and where the problem is written. They may
need a suggested structure for the record.

## Level 2 — distinguish

Explain the difference between prediction, action, cost, metric, and objective. The builder
supplies the reasoning and one rejected alternative.

## Level 3 — inspect evidence

Have the builder identify a label's source and bias, check a data licence, and interpret a
real distribution before you explain it.

## Level 4 — make an engineering case

Require a prior prediction, an unchanged wrong hypothesis when evidence refutes it, a split
justified by deployment, and a control that could disprove the proposed model.

## Level 5 — connect the system

Ask the builder to distinguish a score, calibrated probability, threshold, and action; to
identify duplicate evidence; to cost an option before building; and to explain abstention.

## Level 6 — extend independently

The builder sets scope, records failure modes before failures, researches a specific doubt,
and leaves a handoff with remaining risk stated plainly.

Do not assume the phase number proves readiness. If the current reasoning needs more
context, slow down and return to the concrete incident.
