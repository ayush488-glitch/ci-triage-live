# Rubric ladder

Six levels. Use the level the manifest gives for the current phase. Do not apply the
challenge standard to a beginner in phase 00.

## Level 1 — orientation (phase 00)

Can state: what decision the system supports, who acts on it, why a wrong answer costs
something, and where the problem is written down. You supply most of the structure and may
draft the record. Catching an AI mistake is optional.

## Level 2 — guided understanding (phases 01, 02)

Can distinguish: prediction from action, action from cost, a metric from an objective, and
a number that describes ranking from a number that describes a threshold. Can say why
94% accuracy on a 3% positive rate is not an achievement. You explain the concepts; they
supply the reasoning and one rejected proposal.

## Level 3 — evidence over assertion (phases 03, 04)

Can say where a label came from and what procedure produced it, and can name a bias in
that procedure. Can check a licence before using data. Can look at a real distribution and
say what it forbids. They interpret first; you explain after.

## Level 4 — engineering judgement (phases 05, 06, 07)

Can: predict a result before running it, keep a hypothesis that turned out wrong instead of
quietly editing it, defend a split from the deployment question, explain why the test set
cannot select anything, and run a control that could have made their own idea look bad.
They provide most of the reasoning. You challenge it. One graded interview at phase 06.

## Level 5 — system judgement (phases 08–11)

Can: separate a raw score, a calibrated probability, a threshold, and an action; explain
why two components agreeing is not two pieces of evidence; decide when a component should
be removed rather than improved; cost a design in money and latency before building it;
and say what the system should do when it does not know. Graded interview at phase 09.

## Level 6 — independent extension (phase 12 and the challenge)

Owns the scope. Writes the failure register before the failure. Locates prior work and
says what it changed. Hands the system to another engineer with the remaining risk stated,
not hidden. You question and review; you do not create the judgement.

---

Movement between levels is not automatic with phase number. If a learner is answering at
level 2 in phase 06, say so, and slow down. The phase minutes in the manifest are a budget,
not a schedule.
